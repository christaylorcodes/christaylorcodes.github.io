Styling Considerations

Why Light Blue + Emerald Green?

  • Cool Harmony: Both colors in the cool spectrum (blue/green) create cohesive, professional aesthetic
  • Visual Hierarchy: Light blue (#38bdf8) is brighter, draws initial attention to first name
  • Emotional Appeal: Blue = trust/technology, Green = growth/innovation
  • Perfect for Your Brand: Automation + Operations = technology that enables growth
  • No Warm Colors: Avoids the amber/orange tones you didn't like
  • Excellent Contrast: Both colors pop against dark background
  • Modern & Fresh: Contemporary without being flashy

Implementation

Once you've selected your preferred combination, I'll update the styles in _sass/oceanic/_hero.scss

The changes will be applied to the .highlight-primary and .highlight-secondary classes.
