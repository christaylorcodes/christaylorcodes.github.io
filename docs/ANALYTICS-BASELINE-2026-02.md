## Baseline Summary

### Key Takeaways

1. **Search visibility is growing** - Impressions grew from ~12/day to ~100+/day over 90 days, a 10x increase
2. **CTR needs improvement** - 0.52% overall CTR indicates most rankings are below position 10; improving meta descriptions and targeting better positions could double clicks
3. **ConnectWise content drives traffic** - The Troubleshooting Automate Agents post generates 51% of all impressions; content diversification needed
4. **Long-tail keywords are working** - Specific queries like "connectwise rmm agent heartbeat interval" (position 1.1) show the site can rank for targeted terms
5. **Desktop audience dominates** - 93% of clicks from desktop, typical for technical audience
6. **Global reach achieved** - 125 countries with impressions in 90 days
7. **Homepage SEO is solid** - 4.8% CTR and position 2.8 for branded queries

### Opportunities for Next 90 Days (Feb - May 2026)

1. **Improve CTR on top pages** - Troubleshooting post has 2,977 impressions but only 0.44% CTR; better meta description could yield 15+ additional clicks/month
2. **Target "connectwise automate" broad term** - 103 impressions at position 62; create focused content to improve ranking
3. **Diversify beyond ConnectWise** - 11 of 14 posts are ConnectWise-focused; broader PowerShell/MSP content needed
4. **Improve mobile experience** - Mobile CTR (0.20%) is 3x worse than desktop (0.59%)
5. **Build backlinks** - Domain authority needed to compete for broader terms (Sprint 9)
6. **Publish consistently** - 2+ posts per month to feed Google fresh content (Sprint 12)
